How We Work

A considered process, from first hello to full recovery

We don't rush decisions. Every plan is built around your anatomy, your goals and an honest conversation about what a procedure can — and cannot — do.

  1. 01

    Private consultation

    A one-to-one assessment with your surgeon — in English, Bahasa Malaysia or 中文 — to understand your goals, review your medical history and discuss realistic outcomes.

  2. 02

    Personalised plan

    We map a treatment plan to your anatomy, explain the expected results and recovery, and provide transparent, itemised pricing before you commit to anything.

  3. 03

    The procedure

    Your treatment is carried out in an accredited, sterile facility by an experienced practitioner, with anaesthesia and monitoring appropriate to each procedure.

  4. 04

    Aftercare & follow-up

    Structured aftercare with scheduled reviews. We stay reachable on WhatsApp throughout your recovery — as our patients' own messages on this page show.
