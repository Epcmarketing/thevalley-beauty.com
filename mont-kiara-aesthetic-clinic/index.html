Mont Kiara Clinic

Aesthetic & Cosmetic Surgery Clinic in Mont Kiara

Premium aesthetic medicine and cosmetic surgery in the heart of Mont Kiara, Kuala Lumpur — minutes from Solaris Mont Kiara, 1 Mont Kiara and Plaza Mont Kiara. English, Bahasa Malaysia and Chinese consultations available.

01

A trusted aesthetic clinic for Mont Kiara residents

Mont Kiara is one of Kuala Lumpur's most international neighbourhoods, home to families and professionals who expect refined, clinic-grade aesthetic care. Our medical spa serves the Mont Kiara community from our flagship in Desa Sri Hartamas — a short drive from Solaris Mont Kiara, 1 Mont Kiara, Mont Kiara 2, Plaza Mont Kiara, Verve Suites and the Garden International School. Consultations are unhurried, results are planned around your anatomy and lifestyle, and every step is explained in English, Bahasa Malaysia or Mandarin Chinese.

02

Treatments offered to Mont Kiara patients

From precise cosmetic surgery to advanced non-surgical skin therapies, we offer a complete menu of treatments to Mont Kiara patients. Surgical options include liposuction, rhinoplasty, eye treatment, facelift, breast implant and chin implant. Non-surgical care includes body sculpting, HIFU lifting, pico laser, hair removal, cell light therapy and a whitening drip. Browse all options on the full treatments page.

03

Why Mont Kiara patients choose The Valley

Our team brings more than ten years of cosmetic surgery and aesthetic medicine experience to every consultation, with patients from across Mont Kiara, Damansara Heights, Bukit Tunku and Bangsar. We pair clinical precision with a quiet, spa-like environment, transparent itemised pricing and structured aftercare that stays reachable on WhatsApp. Patient stories are visible across our results and reviews sections.

04

Getting to our clinic from Mont Kiara

From Mont Kiara, our Desa Sri Hartamas clinic is a 5–10 minute drive depending on traffic. Take Jalan Kiara down through Plaza Mont Kiara, continue along Jalan Sri Hartamas 1 and turn into Jalan 30/70A — we are at Lot 22-1, Wisma Rapid, opposite the Hartamas commercial belt. Grab and ride-hailing services reach us within minutes, and complimentary parking is available within the Wisma Rapid building.

FAQ — Mont Kiara

Do you offer consultations in English, Chinese or Malay?
Yes. Our team consults in English, Bahasa Malaysia and Mandarin Chinese, so you can discuss treatments comfortably in your own language.
How long does it take to reach the clinic from Mont Kiara?
From Mont Kiara, the clinic is a 5–10 minute drive depending on traffic. The clinic sits at Lot 22-1, Wisma Rapid, Jalan 30/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas.
Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
We see patients by appointment so we can plan an unhurried, private consultation. Book on WhatsApp or use the contact form and our team will confirm a time that suits you.
